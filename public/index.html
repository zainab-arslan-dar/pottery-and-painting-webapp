The collection

Made slowly, in layers.

Every vase starts as plain terracotta. I trowel on plaster, scrape it back, wash it with earth-toned pigment and press real gold leaf into the cracks. Canvases follow the same rhythm: texture first, colour second, gold last.

Because the process is hands-on, no two pieces match. To buy one, send an inquiry and I'll reply personally with shipping and payment details.
